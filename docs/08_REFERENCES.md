# References and access

Source verification began on 1 November 2026. This project-focused list does not claim exhaustive coverage. Paper PDFs are not included. Links point to primary sources. Before final citation, check current metadata and whether a later version exists.

1. Nikolaidis, N. et al. (2025). **PolyNarrative: A Multilingual, Multilabel, Multi-domain Dataset for Narrative Extraction from News Articles.** ACL, pp. 31323–31345. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.1513. https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1513/. Data: https://nikon95.github.io/narrative-detection/. The authors' access page specifies registration and approval through SemEval Task 10.

2. Upravitelev, M. et al. (2026). **From Repetition to Recognition: Inductive Discovery of Disinformation Narratives.** arXiv:2609.11128v1, preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11128. Code and candidates: https://github.com/XplaiNLP/narrative-discovery. Released narrative candidates should not automatically be treated as new gold labels.

3. Lucas, J. et al. (2023). **Fighting Fire with Fire: The Dual Role of LLMs in Crafting and Detecting Elusive Disinformation.** EMNLP, pp. 14279–14305. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.883. https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.883/. Code: https://github.com/mickeymst/F3.

4. Hamilton, S., Wilkens, M., & Piper, A. (2026). **NarraBench: A Comprehensive Framework for Narrative Benchmarking.** EACL, pp. 3786–3801. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2026.eacl-long.176. https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.176/.

5. Sosnowski, W., Modzelewski, A., Skorupska, K., & Wierzbicki, A. (2025). **DiNaM: Disinformation Narrative Mining with Large Language Models.** EMNLP, pp. 30224–30251 in the downloaded PDF. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1537/. Code: https://github.com/wsosnowski/DiNaM. The study specifies separate use conditions for EDMO fact-checking articles.

6. Piskorski, J. et al. (2025). **SemEval 2025 Task 10: Multilingual Characterization and Extraction of Narratives from Online News.** SemEval, pp. 2610–2643. https://aclanthology.org/2025.semeval-1.331/. Task site: https://propaganda.math.unipd.it/semeval2025task10/.

7. Younus, A., & Qureshi, M. A. (2025). **nlptuducd at SemEval-2025 Task 10: Narrative Classification as a Retrieval Task through Story Embeddings.** SemEval, pp. 1742–1746. https://aclanthology.org/2025.semeval-1.228/.

8. Liang, P. et al. (2023). **Holistic Evaluation of Language Models.** Transactions on Machine Learning Research. arXiv:2211.09110v2. https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110. Section 4.9 on efficiency is particularly relevant.

9. Sosnowski, W., Modzelewski, A., Skorupska, K., Otterbacher, J., & Wierzbicki, A. (2024). **EUDisinfoTest: a Benchmark for Evaluating Language Models’ Ability to Detect Disinformation Narratives.** Findings of EMNLP, pp. 14702–14723. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2024.findings-emnlp.862. https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.862/. An additional corpus with a different task, not a drop-in replacement for paragraph-level narrative extraction.

10. Pham, C. M. et al. (2024). **TopicGPT: A Prompt-based Topic Modeling Framework.** NAACL, pp. 2956–2984. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2024.naacl-long.164. https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.164/. Distinguish topic modeling from narrative classification.

11. **AI & Society Coding Lab / Advanced Study Projects kickoff.** User-supplied `26_10_CODING_LAB_KICKOFF.pdf`, 14 slides. Slides 7, 9 and 11–13 contain formalities, P6 milestones, compute guidance, next steps and starting literature. Kickoff compute recommendations do not verify current service availability.

12. **OpenAI API reference: Create chat completion.** https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/chat/subresources/completions/methods/create. Checked on 1 November 2026. Used as the example adapter's interface contract.

13. **OpenAI documentation: Structured model outputs.** https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs?api-mode=chat. Checked on 1 November 2026. JSON mode does not replace application-level evidence validation.

## Citation checks

Verify titles, dates, venues and versions against canonical pages. Do not cite search snippets as empirical evidence without consulting the study. Page numbers above are publication pages, not PDF positions. For a reading log, record both section and PDF-page references when useful to the group.
